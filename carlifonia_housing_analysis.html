Findings: What Drives House Value?¶

1. Income is the strongest numeric predictor. Median income correlates with the house value at 0.69 - far stronger than any other numeric feature (room count, house age, population, etc.). Higher income areas reliably have higher homw values, though the relationship isn't perfectly linear - at higher incomes, prices vary more widely, suggesting other factors (location, house age) matter more once a baseline income levele is met.

2. Location matters - but "ISLAND" is a statistical trap. Grouping by ocean proximity shows INLAND homes are consistently the cheapest category, averaging ~$124,000 - roughly half the price of any coastal category. NEAR BAY, NEAR OCEAN, and <1H OCEAN cluster closely together in the $240,000-$260,000 range.

ISLAND shows the highest average ($380,440), but this is based on only 5 properties out of 20, 640 - too small a sample to draw a reliable conclusion. This is flaggged rather than treated as a genuine finding.

3. Data Caveat. House value in this dataset are capped at $500,01 - visible as a flat line of repeated maximum value in both the scatter plot and boxplot outliers. This slightly understates the true spread of high-end home prices and should be noted for anyone using this dataset for further modeling.
